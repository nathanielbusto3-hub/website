Artist Statement

My artwork studies how urban decay and natural resilience themes interact with each other through human perception of time. I believe art should connect between human subconscious mind and physical world because it needs to express temporary feelings through permanent artistic works. I direct viewers to discover beauty within everyday objects by emphasizing how light interacts with aged surfaces. I use oil paint and found textiles in my work because I develop my art through intuitive gesture painting which I later refine to represent natural processes of growth and erosion. I want my audience to experience self-reflection through my work which provides them with an immersive journey that will transform their understanding of understanding things.
